## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Ask for lens type when a custom lens has two distinct powers

**Current behavior:**
When a technician enters two lens powers for a custom lens, there is no follow-up question about
lens type.

**Desired behavior:**
Entering two distinct lens powers for a custom lens triggers a follow-up question asking for
lens type, with admin-controlled options sourced from the new Lens Type reference-data category
(Bifocal / Progressive / Other, with free text for Other).

**Key interfaces:**
- The custom-lens entry form — a conditional field keyed on whether two distinct powers have
  been entered, sourcing its options from the Lens Type reference-data category.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Entering two distinct powers for a custom lens shows a lens-type question with the
      admin-configured options.
- [ ] Entering a single power does not show this question.
- [ ] Choosing "Other" reveals a free-text field, consistent with the existing `IsOtherOption`
      pattern used elsewhere.

**Out of scope:**
- Defining the reference-data category itself — tracked separately (ticket 13). Do not start this
  ticket until that category exists.
