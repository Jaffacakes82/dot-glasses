## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Route to the Lead flow instead of allowing "save test" once contact details are
captured

**Current behavior:**
The record-a-test screen allows a technician to save a plain Test even after the customer has
shared contact details, rather than requiring those details be captured as part of a proper
Lead.

**Desired behavior:**
Once any contact-detail field has been entered on the record-a-test screen, "save test" is no
longer available in that form — the technician is routed into the Lead flow instead, so contact
details are always captured as part of a Lead rather than risking being discarded on a plain
Test save.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Test recording form/flow — the presence of contact-detail input should gate which save
  action is available, consistent with the existing Test→Lead relationship
  (`Test.ConvertedToLeadId` / `Lead.SourceTestId`).

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] With no contact details entered, "save test" behaves as it does today.
- [ ] Once any contact-detail field is filled in, the technician is routed into the Lead flow
      instead of being able to save a plain Test.
- [ ] No contact detail entered by a technician is ever silently discarded by a plain Test save.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing the underlying Test→Lead conversion mechanics beyond this entry-point routing.
