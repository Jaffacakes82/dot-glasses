**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Make PD (pupillary distance) optional when recording a Lead

**Current behavior:**
PD is a required field on the Lead form.

**Desired behavior:**
PD becomes optional — a Lead can be saved without it, since there's often no time to measure it
during a busy event or day.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Lead request DTO/validator's PD field — relax the required-field rule client- and
  server-side.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A Lead can be submitted successfully with PD left blank.
- [ ] A Lead with PD provided still saves and displays it as before.
- [ ] Any downstream logic that currently assumes PD is always present (display, export, etc.)
      handles a missing value gracefully rather than erroring.

**Out of scope:**
