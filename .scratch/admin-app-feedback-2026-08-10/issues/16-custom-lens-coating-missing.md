## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Coating field intermittently fails to appear after switching a lens selection to Custom in the Field App consultation form

**Current behavior:**
The lens-range selector component owns the lens range type (preset vs. custom) and mutates it
directly on a shared model object when the user switches ranges. It does not notify its parent
(the consultation form) that the range type changed. The parent's Coating/Coating-preference
field is shown conditionally based on that same range type, but because it isn't told to
re-render, the condition is only re-evaluated the next time *some other* field on the parent
happens to trigger a render — not immediately when the range switches to Custom. The practical
symptom: a technician switches to Custom and the Coating field appears to be missing, until they
touch an unrelated field on the form, at which point it shows up.

**Desired behavior:**
The Coating field (Sale path) and Coating preference field (Lead path) should appear immediately
and reliably the moment the lens range type changes to Custom — no dependency on touching any
other field first. Switching away from Custom should hide/update the field just as immediately.

**Key interfaces:**
- The lens-range-selector component needs an explicit change notification (e.g. an
  `EventCallback` fired whenever the range type mutates) so its parent can react and re-render,
  rather than relying on the parent's own incidental re-renders.
- The parent's render logic that conditionally shows the coating field(s) based on lens range
  type should be triggered by that notification.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Switching a lens selection to Custom immediately shows the Coating dropdown on the Sale
      recording path, with no other field needing to change first.
- [ ] Switching a lens selection to Custom immediately shows the Coating preference dropdown on
      the Lead recording path (where applicable), with no other field needing to change first.
- [ ] Switching away from Custom back to a preset range immediately hides/updates the coating
      field(s) accordingly.
- [ ] No regression to the SixLensSet/NineLensSet paths, which intentionally don't show the
      optional coating-preference field on the Lead path.

**Out of scope:**
- Which coatings are actually selectable/compatible for custom vs. preset lenses — that's a
  separate, larger design item (lens-coating compatibility rules) tracked elsewhere in this
  feedback batch.
- Any restructuring of the lens-range selector beyond adding the change notification needed to
  fix this bug.
