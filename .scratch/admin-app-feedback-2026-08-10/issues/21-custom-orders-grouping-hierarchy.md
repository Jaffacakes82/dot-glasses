**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Group Custom Orders by retailer, then retail point, then customer name

**Current behavior:**
The Custom Orders screen lists orders without a retailer → retail point → customer grouping, and
without per-retailer/retail-point active-order totals.

**Desired behavior:**
Custom Orders are displayed grouped primarily by retailer, then by retail point within that
retailer, then by customer name — so a country office can see which custom lenses were ordered
for delivery to a given retailer or retail point, and a retailer can see custom lenses ordered by
the retail points they run, within existing hierarchy-scoping rules (unchanged). Each retailer
and retail point grouping shows a count of its active custom lens orders.

- The Custom Orders list view/query — the grouping structure should reflect `OrganisationNode`'s
  hierarchy relationships (retailer as an `Intermediate` node, retail points as its `RetailPoint`
  children), respecting the data-scoping rules already applied to this screen.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [x] Custom Orders are grouped by retailer, then retail point, then customer name, in that
      order.
- [x] Each retailer and retail point grouping shows a count of its active custom lens orders.
- [x] A user only sees groupings/orders within their existing hierarchy scope, unchanged.

- The retailer-vs-retail-point grouping toggle — explicitly flagged by the reporter as possible
