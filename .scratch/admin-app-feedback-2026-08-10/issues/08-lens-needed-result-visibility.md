## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Show the lens-needed test result field whenever a person needs glasses, not only
once they become a Lead

**Current behavior:**
The field recording which lens(es) are needed appears to be gated on the record reaching Lead
status (or a similarly downstream state), rather than on the Test's own "needs glasses" outcome.

**Desired behavior:**
Whenever a Test records that the person needs glasses, the lens-needed result field is available
to record — regardless of whether that Test goes on to convert into a Lead. The full set of lens
options available elsewhere, including custom, should be selectable here too.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Test recording flow/form — the condition gating this field should key off the Test's own
  "needs glasses" outcome, not off Lead-conversion status.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Recording a Test where the person needs glasses shows the lens-needed field, even when the
      technician doesn't go on to capture Lead contact details.
- [ ] The field offers the same full set of lens options available elsewhere, including custom.
- [ ] Behavior for a Test where the person does not need glasses is unchanged.

**Out of scope:**
- Changing what counts as "needs glasses" — only this field's visibility gating changes.
